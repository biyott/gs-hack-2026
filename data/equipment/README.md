# Equipment catalog

`catalog.json` version 1.0.1 is generated by `node data/equipment/build-catalog.mjs`. Inputs are the preserved SK template, four canonical rig fragments under `resources/blender/safety-simulator/`, and the manufacturer archive manifest. `catalog.v1.0.0.json` preserves the earlier baseline. Shared Zod validation lives in `packages/contracts/src/catalog.ts` and `articulation.ts`; snapshot wire version remains 1.0.0.

All six goal 008 presets are retained. Official `lengthM/widthM/heightM` are identified by `dimensionState`; unknowns remain null. Working visual extents and chosen demo poses are separate. Support points remain null where oriented manufacturer points are unconfirmed, even when a synthetic rendered arrangement exists. The 172's 4.6 m source claim is retained as nominal support width; its exact outer envelope remains unknown.

These are tested model kinematic ranges, not manufacturer operating limits or lift plans. SK's original 60 m jib configuration and the selected 172 configuration remain fixed. Neither selected lattice assembly gains a telescoping control. SK's 34 m visual hook limit preserves cable clearance while the published maximum 35 m remains in official metadata. Only SK receives the goal's table slew limit.

Four entries have typed `articulation`: local-Z luffing, nested local-X stage extension, hook world height and physical-tip horizontal position, plus normalized local-Y endpoint links. Preserve initial loaded transforms and apply deltas from `controlBaselines`. Recompute links after each pose change. SK and 172 retain their verified relative trolley/hook/rope handling. Never move a hook without its linked rope.

`riskGeometry` contains separate synthetic polygons for modeled body, supports, rotating tail and suspended load in chassis/upper/hook frames. They are not manufacturer-certified exclusion zones. Hook-frame offsets retain actual boom heel offsets (LR +1.2 m; Tadano −1.1 m; LTM −1.8 m). TD/LTM physical sheave offsets are bounded by 0.05 m and covered by the chosen load polygon. `riskGeometryStatus` is verified after engine-owner validation of the chosen polygon model and declared motions (148 tests). This does not certify the entire visual mesh footprint or manufacturer mechanics. Source/export tests and final independent QA are separate gates.

Asset sources, reproduction instructions and executed evidence are indexed in `resources/blender/safety-simulator/README.md`. No model-specific code is required to decode the four typed rigs.
