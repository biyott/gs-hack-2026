## Apparatus and prerequisites

- Use assigned real Controller/Controlee phones and exact final APK/server. Record phone aliases, OS/API, role, runtime session, whether actual angular samples are available, and the source of every observation. Device-model names and feature lists do not prove AoA availability.
- Mount the Controller upright in portrait on a stable fixture so its angular measurement plane is horizontal relative to the table. Independently observe/measure pose using the apparatus reference, level or surveyed geometry; record actual pitch/roll and the method. Do not infer physical mounting from screen orientation, orientation lock or an emulator.
- Mount the visual marker separately on a rigid horizontal platform parallel to the table while the handset remains upright. Document how the platform is rigidly attached and its orientation relative to the phone. Photograph/measure the arrangement, marker center/height, antenna reference/height, equipment slew reference and their offsets. A marker attached flush to an upright phone back is not a horizontal table-plane marker.
- Define the marker's heading in the calibrated table frame and an independently surveyed physical ray normal to the handset back. Keep `uwbYawRad` null until actual zero/sign/orientation calibration is confirmed. Zero is a measured value only when supported by evidence, never a convenient default.
- Arbitrary pitch/roll or a flat handset is outside the reported yaw-only projection model. Do not force such a mounting into a yaw correction or claim its angular plane is horizontal. Preserve raw distance/angles and truthful unavailable projection; video tracking remains separately sourced.
